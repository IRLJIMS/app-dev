#  One Punch Man


## 📖 Overview
**One Punch Man** is a Japanese superhero anime and manga series created by **ONE**.  
It tells the story of **Saitama**, a hero who can defeat any opponent with a single punch — yet feels *bored and unfulfilled* because no one can challenge him.

---

## 🦸‍♂️ Main Characters
1. **Saitama** – The unbeatable hero who trained so hard that he lost his hair.  
2. **Genos** – A powerful cyborg and Saitama’s loyal disciple.  
3. **Speed-o'-Sound Sonic** – A fast and prideful ninja.  
4. **Mumen Rider** – A powerless hero with a strong sense of justice.  

---

## ⚔️ Favorite Moments
> *“I’m just a guy who’s a hero for fun.”* — **Saitama**

- When Saitama destroys enemies effortlessly, leaving everyone in awe.
